The experience behind it

What led me here

I did not want to keep spending so much time repeating data extraction, Excel organization, and cleaning. Looking for a more efficient method led me to become interested in AI, technology, and coding. For me, that interest started with a practical question: how could I make this work more productive?

Identify the repetition

I look for tasks that repeat and consider whether a workflow can make them easier to carry out.

Build around a useful process

I use automation to connect recurring steps once their purpose and information needs are clear.

Keep judgment human

The aim is to be more productive and leave more attention for verification, interpretation, and decisions.

Why I put this stage here

Productivity matters when the saved effort can go into better thinking and more useful work. Automating a task does not remove responsibility for its results.

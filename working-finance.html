Define the goal

I decide what the project needs to explain or support: a cash requirement, a valuation, or another specific financial decision.

Choose a project-specific strategy

I shape the approach around that goal, the business context, and the evidence available.

Keep the purpose visible

I use the goal to decide which assumptions and outputs deserve attention, and what the project can responsibly conclude.

Why I put this stage here

A method is useful when it answers the right question. Setting the goal first gives the analysis a purpose and makes its limitations easier to explain.

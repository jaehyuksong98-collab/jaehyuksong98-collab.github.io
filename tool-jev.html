What I mean by Jev

Here I mean askjev.ai, Wayne Sutton’s demo built around TypeSafe’s Jev model. Its terms describe a public question wall, with optional model-written replies. It is not the same kind of tool as a full financial research workspace.

What I would learn from it

I would explore how a short question is classified and sent to a suitable model. For finance work, the useful idea is separating simple sorting from tasks that need deeper review.

A small practice task

I would start with invented, non-sensitive examples: is a note about revenue, costs, or financing? I would label the examples myself, compare the tool’s choices, and review mistakes. I would not automate requests to the public demo.

What stays outside it

Anonymous and public asks appear on the wall; even private asks can be viewed by the operator. I would keep company records and personal information out. A yes/no answer is not a basis for a financial decision.

Original commentary

As major technology firms dramatically scale capital expenditure on AI infrastructure, financial markets price in unprecedented short-term productivity gains and margin expansions. However, for a finance specialist like Jaehyuk Song, the long-term systemic risk lies in the disruption of the macroeconomic circular flow: if AI-driven intellectual automation permanently displaces high-wage service sectors, aggregate consumer purchasing power will collapse, eventually erasing the corporate revenues that justify today's valuations. To sustain long-term market demand, institutional investors must shift focus from immediate cost-cutting metrics to broader wealth distribution mechanisms, such as sovereign wealth funds and democratized asset ownership.

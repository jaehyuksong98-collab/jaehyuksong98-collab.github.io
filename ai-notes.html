Attitudes & learning

Stay curious.
Put learning to work.

How I learn, apply new knowledge, and use AI with my own judgment.

Learn, connect, apply.

When I learn something, I constantly look for more efficient ways to do it—like automating repetitive tasks and putting them straight into real use. Connecting new things to actual work is the only way I truly remember them and get more done.

Use AI. Keep judgment.

As AI gets smarter, people think human decisions matter less, but I believe the opposite. What really counts is how reliably and sharply you can guide AI. I work like a manager: I direct the tool, double-check the results, and take ownership of the final calls.

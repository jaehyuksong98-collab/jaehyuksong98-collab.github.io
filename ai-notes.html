01 / Learning in practice

Learn, connect, apply.

My interest in AI, technology, and coding started with repetitive work: extracting data, organizing it in Excel, and cleaning it. I wanted to spend less time repeating those steps and find a better way to work.

When I learn something, I look for a way to connect it to what I already know and put it straight into use. That is how I remember it, see what works, and get more done.

02 / AI & judgment

Use AI. Keep judgment.

As AI gets smarter, I believe human judgment matters more. I work like a manager: I set the direction, guide the tool, double-check the results, and take ownership of the final calls.

I check data against its source and cross-check important claims. A confident answer can still be wrong. The final work needs to sound like me and reflect what I actually understand. Knowing the subject—and knowing how to use the information—matters more, not less.

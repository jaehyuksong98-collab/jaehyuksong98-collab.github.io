My direction

Start small. Check the output. Keep responsibility.

Astra, Opus 5.5 & Ask Jev ↗Official service information ↗

The role I would give it

I would use Grok as one place to explore questions raised by market news and public discussion. A popular claim can be a starting point for research, but it is not evidence on its own.

How I would guide it

I would ask it to separate the event, the original source, other people’s opinions, and unanswered questions. Dates matter: an old report should not become today’s news just because it is being shared again.

What I would check

I would verify market-moving claims against company filings, official releases, or reliable reporting. Screenshots, reposts, and confident summaries would not become model inputs without that check.

Bring it back to the company

I would ask which variable could actually change: demand, price, cost, delivery time, or funding. Then I would test that variable in the model instead of copying a market opinion.

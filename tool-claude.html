The role I would give it

I would use Claude to question my assumptions, review a model explanation, and help inspect code. I want it to find where my reasoning is weak, not simply agree with my conclusion.

How I would guide it

I would give it the objective, source documents, assumptions, and calculations separately. I would ask which conclusions the evidence supports, which need more data, and which change under another reasonable assumption.

What I would check

I would open the cited pages and reproduce the calculations. When two AI tools agree, I would still check the source: agreement between models is not independent proof.

Keep my own voice

I would ask for plain language, then rewrite the final note myself. If I cannot explain a sentence or calculation, it is not ready to publish.

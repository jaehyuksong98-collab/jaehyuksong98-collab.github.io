Copilot

Make spreadsheet work easier to inspect.

My direction

Start small. Check the output. Keep responsibility.

Astra, Opus 5.5 & Ask Jev ↗Official service information ↗

The role I would give it

I would test Microsoft Copilot for help with Excel formulas, explaining a table, and preparing a short summary from a checked workbook. I would first confirm which features are available in my account.

How I would guide it

I would describe the column meanings, units, date range, and expected result before asking for a formula or chart. I would use a copy of the workbook so that I can compare changes.

What I would check

I would inspect cell references, filters, signs, blanks, and the treatment of dates. I would calculate a few rows myself and compare totals before using the result.

A clear limit

Copilot could help me work through a spreadsheet, but I would still choose the assumptions and explain the model. A suggested formula is something to test, not something to trust immediately.

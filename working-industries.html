01 / Industries

Understand the business before the numbers.

Map the industry, its leading companies, buyers, and suppliers.

01 / Industries02 / Finance03 / Data04 / Automation

Start with the industry structure

Before I build a model, I work out how the industry operates: its main activities, how businesses create value, and how money moves through the system.

Identify the leading companies

I identify the largest companies and distinguish their roles and business models. This helps me understand the competitive landscape and place the company I am studying in context.

Define the buyers and suppliers

I map the main customers and suppliers, what they exchange, and how they depend on one another. I want to understand what drives demand and what shapes supply before interpreting the numbers.

Turn that understanding into questions

I use this industry map to identify the questions that matter for the project. Those questions give the financial analysis a starting point grounded in how the business actually works.

Why I put this stage here

The same figure can mean different things in different businesses. Understanding how the industry works helps me decide what a number can actually tell me.

A related project: liquidity and payment timing

The 13-week model distinguishes profit from the timing of receipts and payments. Its case study shows why the flow of money matters to the question being asked.

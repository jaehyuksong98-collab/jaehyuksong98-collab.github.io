Read the context

I start with the industry and the situation around a project. I want to understand what the business does and what influences it.

Follow the money

I look at how value is created, who pays, and where the money goes. This gives financial information a business context.

Frame the next question

That context helps me decide which questions the project should answer and which information matters.

Why I put this stage here

The same figure can mean different things in different businesses. Understanding how the industry works helps me decide what a number can actually tell me.

Learning the ideas behind the numbers

At BI, I have been building my knowledge of finance, accounting, economics, and data analysis. It has helped me understand the thinking behind a model, not just how to fill in a spreadsheet.

I like connecting what I learn to something I can use. Sometimes that means building a model. Sometimes it means looking back at work I did before university and realizing how I could approach it differently now.

The people are part of the experience

University has also been a place to make friends and meet people with different interests and backgrounds. I have made an effort to build relationships alongside my studies.

I joined the AI Club and Case Club because I wanted to learn with other people and get involved beyond my classes. For me, networking starts with being curious about people, having conversations, and staying in touch.

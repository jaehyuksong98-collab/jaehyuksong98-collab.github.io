Learning fine dining—and learning from someone who is good with people.

BI Norwegian Business SchoolJ2 · OsloBatwostay · SeoulReal estate agency · Seoul

Getting used to fine dining

Working at J2 has introduced me to the culture of a fine dining restaurant. I have learned to pay attention to the details of service and to how guests feel throughout a meal.

It is work I do alongside my studies, but I do not see it as separate from what I am learning. It gives me a different kind of practice: communicating clearly, working with a team, and responding to people in the moment.

Learning from the owner

I feel lucky to work with an owner who is very good at communicating with people. Even when English or Norwegian is a challenge, he finds a way to connect with guests and make them feel comfortable.

Watching him has taught me a lot. It has made me think about communication as more than speaking a language perfectly. Paying attention to people and how you make them feel matters too.

What I want to carry into other work

I want to bring that same care into business. Having the right information is useful, but so is being able to listen, explain it clearly, and help the other person feel comfortable asking a question.

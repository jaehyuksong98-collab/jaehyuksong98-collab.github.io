My experience · 2020—2021

Real estate agency · Seoul

Helping people understand what they were signing.

BI Norwegian Business SchoolJ2 · OsloBatwostay · SeoulReal estate agency · Seoul

Learning the market and the rules

Studying for the Korean licensed real estate agent qualification gave me a broad foundation in how the property market works and the laws around transactions. Working in an agency helped me see how that knowledge was used in practice.

At Wonheung Station Prugio Real Estate Agency, I worked with transaction documents and client and property records. I also learned about sales through the conversations that happen around a property decision.

A signed contract did not always mean peace of mind

Many clients were still anxious after signing a contract because they were not fully sure what they had signed or what would happen next. That stayed with me.

I tried to explain things in simple words, make people comfortable asking questions, and keep them updated as the process moved forward. I wanted them to understand what was happening, rather than feel that they had to pretend they understood.

What I learned about trust

I learned about property, but the bigger lesson for me was communication and building relationships. Knowing the details is only part of the job. Being able to explain them in a way that helps someone feel informed and less worried matters just as much.

That is something I want to keep doing in finance and other business work: explain clearly, give accurate updates, and make it easier for people to ask when something is unclear.

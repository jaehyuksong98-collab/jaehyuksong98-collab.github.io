Attitudes & learning / AI & judgment

Better tools. More judgment.

Why data verification, my own voice, and human decisions matter more as AI becomes more capable.

2026-09-22BY JAEHYUK SONG

Keep exploring

All AI notes ↗AI workflow project ↗

Verify the data

I treat AI-generated facts and numbers as information to check, not evidence on their own.

Question confident answers

I watch for hallucinations and unsupported conclusions. A convincing answer still needs a sound basis.

Make the final work my own

I revise the final output so it reflects my own voice and understanding.

Keep decisions human

AI can support a decision; a person has to make it. Knowledge, judgment, and knowing how to use information matter even more.

About this note

This reflection describes my own attitude and experience. It is not a news report or a claim about a particular tool.

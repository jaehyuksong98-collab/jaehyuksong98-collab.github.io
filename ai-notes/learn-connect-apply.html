Attitudes & learning / Learning in practice

Learn it. Connect it. Put it to work.

My interest in AI, technology, and coding grew from looking for a better way to handle repetitive data work.

2026-09-22BY JAEHYUK SONG

Keep exploring

All AI notes ↗AI workflow project ↗

Start with a practical question

Extracting data, organizing it in Excel, and cleaning it meant spending time on repetitive work. I wanted to find a more efficient method. That search led me to become interested in AI, technology, and coding.

Connect new knowledge to what I know

I am eager to learn something new. When I do, I think about how it connects with the knowledge and skills I already have, and where it could be useful in actual work.

Put it into practice

I want to move from learning to trying. Looking for a practical use, applying what I have learned, and reflecting on the result are part of the attitude I want to bring to my studies and work.

Bring judgment with me

Using a new tool does not remove the need to understand the information it produces. As AI becomes more capable, I see knowledge, verification, and human decision-making as even more important.

About this note

This reflection describes my own attitude and experience. It is not a news report or a claim about a particular tool.

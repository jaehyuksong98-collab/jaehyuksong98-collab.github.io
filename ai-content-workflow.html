AI Content Distribution Workflow

A practical automation prototype connecting source collection, AI-assisted writing, visual production, publishing workflows, and content tracking.

N8N · GEMINI · JAVASCRIPT · GOOGLE SHEETS · PLACID · NOTION

Overview

From source feeds to channel-ready content

I designed and built a scheduled n8n workflow to collect source material from Google Sheets and XML/HTTP feeds, transform it with JavaScript and Gemini, and route outputs into separate Naver Blog and Instagram workflows.

The system connected Placid for visual generation, Notion for content tracking, and Gmail for workflow notifications. The goal was to reduce repetitive communication work while keeping the process visible and reviewable.

Workflow

How the system was structured

1 · CollectSchedule trigger, Google Sheets, XML and HTTP feeds
2 · TransformJavaScript cleaning and field preparation
3 · GenerateGemini-assisted report and channel copy
4 · ProducePlacid visual generation and publishing steps
5 · TrackNotion records and Gmail notifications

What I learned

Designing for reliability across services

  • Breaking a long automation into channel-specific branches makes failures easier to isolate.
  • Structured intermediate data is essential when AI output must feed JavaScript and external APIs.
  • Tracking generated content in Notion creates a review trail instead of treating automation as a black box.
